# Intersection right-of-way — Foundation 120

Open **Inspect Intersection Traffic Paused**. Two cars reach the same marked junction on tick 3. Vehicle #1 enters first; selected vehicle #2 reports **Waiting for intersection**. Step 100 ticks to see both finish their trips. Restart clears the queue and returns to the original paused scenario.

Adjacent marked road cells with multiple legal directions form one junction. Only one car occupies that junction at a time. A waiting car enters on a later movement update after the previous occupant leaves, keeping their smooth movement apart. Before entering, a car traces its planned turn to the first exit cell and checks that pedestrians and traffic are clear, including straight-road following space beyond the exit. A blocked approach reports **Waiting for clear exit**; other approaches with clear exits can still proceed. Pedestrians on the intended path retain priority. A pedestrian entering after admission can still make the car pause inside.

Approaching cars save the tick when they first request entry. The oldest currently clear approach goes first; simultaneous requests use vehicle ID as a deterministic tie-breaker. Requests are recorded before any cars move in that tick. A stopped or retargeted car relinquishes its waiting age. A car already inside holds the junction until it leaves, including when manually stopped. Do not stop or place a parked vehicle in a junction unless you intend to obstruct traffic. New destinations inside junctions are rejected, so normal trips cannot park there.

During playback, the whole vehicle body, windows and wheels rotate smoothly through the shortest angle over its movement interval. Repeated unchanged headings do not restart the turn. Rotation affects presentation only; logical movement remains on road cells. Pausing, loading and manual stepping show the exact committed position and heading. Resume begins from that synchronized pose. Rotated vehicles remain clickable.
