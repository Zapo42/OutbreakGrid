# Foundation 109 — larger home supply district

Choose **Inspect Larger District Paused** from the main menu, then Run. This controlled scenario has no zombies so supply travel can be observed without combat interruptions. Use the existing 5×/10×/20×/50× controls, or step game hours. Restart scenario restores the original stock, assignments, clock and empty order queue.

The 64 × 36 map contains three separate homes, three stores, one distribution center and eleven humans: six consumers, three clerks and two couriers. Every human has an assigned home separate from work. Home chests begin empty; each store shelf has six meals, its supply crate begins empty, and the center starts with 80 meals, including six protected reserve meals. Eleven carried personal rations bring total initial food to **109 meals**. Stock is finite; food is never generated by this scenario.

Stores sit at different distances from the center, with the far store more than 50 columns away. Consumers inspect their home chest, collect from familiar neighborhood shelves and return groceries. Clerks move crate food onto shelves and use phones to request more when crates are empty. The two center couriers share the existing request queue and deliver physical loads. Worker shifts are 07–19; workers finish accepted loads and go to their assigned homes off duty.

The initially selected human is a consumer in the western home. Click other humans to follow their status and target; click chests/crates for inventory. Existing collapsible store and center summaries provide **Observe store** and **Observe center** focus buttons. Tan floors mark homes, blue stores and green the distribution center. Roads and sidewalks remain walking terrain.

Residents begin with a remembered neighborhood shelf from tick zero. These are authored familiar-shop observations, not global live inventory access. The separate **Inspect Store Discovery Paused** preview still tests learning unfamiliar stores. Jobs begin at their workplaces to avoid making all first deliveries wait for a morning commute.

This increment expands the scenario without changing saved rules or routing algorithms. It deliberately keeps destinations within the existing 64-cell assigned-worker/household routing bound. It does not establish travel across arbitrary city distances, driving, traffic, specialized shops or renewable distribution stock. Actual travel still consumes energy; natural idle weight shifts do not.

Automated checks cover finite stock conservation, groceries reaching all three homes, deliveries reaching all three stores, parallel courier claims, and deterministic save/load during long routes. UI API doubles cover the paused menu entry, initial selection, order creation and restart. Unity 6000.6.3f1 import, presentation and native performance remain untested, per the pause after Foundation 82.
