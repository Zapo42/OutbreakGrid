# Foundation 54 / replacement supply trips

Calm humans carrying bats with three or fewer hits remaining can walk to a visible, better bat. The replacement must offer at least three more hits. Routes are limited to six movement steps and cannot open closed doors. Shorter routes take priority; equal routes prefer better condition, then stable map order.

Food, shelter, infection, stress, warnings and visible zombies retain priority. Humans reconsider supplies each opportunity, so another human taking a bat cancels that trip. On arrival, exchanging bats takes a separate action and leaves the old bat on the ground with its original wear. No equipment is repaired or duplicated.

From the main menu choose **Inspect Replacement Trip Paused**, then **Run** or **Step 50 ticks**. The human crosses the corridor, exchanges its worn bat and leaves that bat behind. **Restart scenario** restores the original setup. Existing Foundation 53 saves retain their behavior until **Enable replacement trips** is selected.

Automated checks cover movement, separate swaps, finite equipment, survival priorities, interrupted trips, exact save continuation, older rules, menu controls, upgrades and restart. Native Unity rendering and Windows builds require local verification.
