# Zombie sound investigation

Current build: **Foundation 13 / zombie sound investigation**, rules `encounter-prototype-12`.

Idle zombies now investigate nearby door impacts they actually hear. A sound gives them a temporary door location rather than knowledge of a hidden human.

## Try it

1. Download GitHub **Code → Download ZIP**, extract into a fresh folder and open **OutbreakRebuild** in Unity 6.
2. Choose **Outbreak → Rebuild → Open and Run Foundation**.
3. Choose **Inspect Zombie Sound Paused**. Click zombie **#3**, farther to the right of the attacking zombie.
4. Click **Step 10 ticks**: zombie #3 approaches the heard impacts, and its inspector shows the source location. It has no human target.
5. Click **Step 10 ticks** again: it waits outside the still-closed door. Sound alone does not let it break the door.
6. Two more **Step 10 ticks** reach tick 40: the original attacker broke the door, the listener acquired the human by sight, and ordinary pursuit/contact resumed.

New quick and configured encounters enable zombie investigation automatically. For a Foundation 12 save, use **Load Run → Enable zombie sound → Run**. The upgrade preserves the actors, door damage, clock and random state, starts paused and leaves the source file unchanged.

## Behavior

- Real door strikes use the existing eight-cell acoustic travel budget. Solid walls block paths; doors and windows transmit impacts. Out-of-range or fully enclosed zombies receive no cue.
- The attacker ignores its own strike. Other reachable zombies remember the latest heard impact location for five simulation seconds, refreshed only by another actual impact.
- Decision priority is **visible human → remembered human sighting → recent sound → occasional wandering**. Hearing does not replace a zombie's remembered pursuit target.
- A sound investigator uses normal collision and crowd limits. A closed door or transmitting window can block its physical route. Hearing alone does not grant a human ID, permit door attacks or reveal hidden human coordinates.
- Reaching an open source cell clears the sound memory. If it cannot reach the source, the memory still expires on its exact tick. Later opportunities return to wandering unless a human is seen or another impact is heard.
- Visual target acquisition, bites, door attacks based on human sightings, staggering, human stress and shelter recovery keep their existing rules. Sound does not add human reaction or stress fields to zombies.

The inspector shows the heard source and countdown. A targetless zombie with recent sound displays **Activity: investigating heard impact**; observed human pursuit retains its own target and memory display.

## Persistence and scope

Recent sound locations, remaining memory and investigation behavior continue exactly after Save/Load Run. Pause stops movement and memory time. No new random draws or event backlog are introduced by hearing or investigation; wandering still uses the saved simulation stream when it becomes eligible again.

Earlier rules keep their original human-only hearing behavior until explicitly upgraded. They reject active zombie hearing records. Independent prototype-eleven reference fixtures were generated by executing commit `18248f7` with an additional idle zombie beside the breach scenario.

This extends simulated hearing behavior. Speaker audio clips, shouts, weapon sounds, group communication and coordinated hordes remain future work. One recent impact source is retained per listener; a newer actual impact can replace it.
