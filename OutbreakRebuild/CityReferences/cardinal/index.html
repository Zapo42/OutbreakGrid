Cardinal cities · replayable seeds and settings

The first three examples rotate a reference layout's dominant street axes toward game north/south and east/west. Secondary angles remain; geographic north is rotated. The following maps generate entirely new, strictly cardinal street networks.

Six presets: compact grid, long blocks, mixed town, town centre, suburban loops and suburban dead ends. The suburban presets are designed examples, not newly downloaded geographic surveys. Repeat a recipe for identical map bytes; change the seed to change streets and lots.

Controls: block counts, block dimensions, variation, connecting loops, dead-end branches, local/arterial road widths, sidewalks, building density, setbacks, open space and centre density. Coverage is a placement target; actual buildings depend on available space. No NPC or traffic behavior is changed.

Extract this pack before opening the HTML. Load its .obmap2 files using Load Map in the game.
